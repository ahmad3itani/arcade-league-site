Delete your account

Last updated September 11, 2026

How to delete your Arcade League account and data.

In the app (instant)

Open Arcade League → Profile → Danger zone → Delete account, and tap again to confirm. Your account and all its data are erased immediately.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email ahmed3itani@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your player name (and your account ID if you have it — it’s on the Profile screen). We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days, then confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile and player name, runs and scores, league history, coins, streaks, chests, duels, cosmetic items, reports you filed, push tokens and any unpaid prize. Records we must keep by law (for example of prizes already paid) are retained only as long as required. Analytics events are deleted on request.
