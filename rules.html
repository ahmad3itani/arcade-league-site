Official Rules — Monthly Diamond Prize

Last updated September 10, 2026

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE WILL NOT IMPROVE YOUR CHANCES. THIS IS A CONTEST OF SKILL. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED. Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of, and are not involved in any way with, this contest.

1. Sponsor

The contest is sponsored by Ahmad Itani ("Sponsor"), the operator of Arcade League. Contact: ahmed3itani@gmail.com. Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of, and are not involved in, this contest in any manner.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of Canada (excluding Quebec) and the United States who are at least the age of majority in their province, territory or state of residence at the time of claiming (for example 18 in Ontario, 19 in British Columbia, 21 in Mississippi). The winner must play on a permanent account linked to Google or Apple. The Sponsor and members of the Sponsor’s household are not eligible. Void in Quebec and wherever prohibited or restricted by law. Anyone may play the game; only eligible players can receive a cash prize.

3. Contest periods

Each calendar month (Coordinated Universal Time, UTC) is a separate contest period. League weeks run Monday 00:00 UTC to the following Monday 00:00 UTC; a week counts toward the month in which it ends.

4. How to enter

Install Arcade League and play league runs. Each league run uses one ticket; tickets are free, you hold up to five, and one regenerates every 25 minutes. Tickets cannot be purchased. There is no other method of entry and none is needed, because entry is free.

5. How winners are determined (skill only)

Every submitted run is re-simulated on the Sponsor’s servers from its recorded inputs; only the server-computed score counts. Each verified score is converted to Arcade Points by comparing it with all verified scores in that game. Your best verified score in each game each day earns Arcade Points; your weekly total adds up those daily results for your best games (the number of games counted is shown in the app and is currently five of seven). At the end of each week, players who finished that week in the Diamond tier add that week’s points to their monthly Diamond total. The ten players with the highest monthly Diamond totals are the potential winners. Ties are broken in favour of the player whose first Diamond week of the month ended earlier, then the player whose account was created earlier. No element of chance determines the winners.

6. Prizes

The total prize pool is US$100 per contest period, split by final rank: 1st US$25, 2nd US$18, 3rd US$14, 4th US$11, 5th US$9, 6th US$7, 7th US$6, 8th US$4, 9th and 10th US$3 each. Prizes are paid by PayPal in US dollars. Prizes are not transferable and may not be exchanged; the Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if PayPal is unavailable to a winner. Winners are responsible for any taxes. One prize per person per contest period.

7. Claiming a prize

Potential winners are notified in the app on the Prize status screen. To claim, a potential winner must, within 60 days after the end of the contest period: (a) be signed in to a linked Google or Apple account; (b) provide their legal name, PayPal email address, country and province/state of residence, and date of birth; (c) correctly answer, without assistance, a mathematical skill-testing question (three attempts; after three incorrect answers the claim is placed with a person for review); and (d) agree to these rules. The Sponsor then reviews the winner’s runs for fair play. Approved prizes are paid within 30 days of approval.

8. Forfeiture

A prize is forfeited, and is not awarded to another player, if the potential winner is ineligible (including by residence or age), plays on an anonymous account, does not complete a valid claim within 60 days, fails the fair-play review, or has provided false information. Forfeited prizes are not redistributed.

9. Fair play and disqualification

Automation, input replay, modified clients, exploiting bugs, multiple accounts, collusion, or submitting runs played by anyone else will result in disqualification and forfeiture. The Sponsor keeps every run’s inputs so any result can be replayed and audited.

10. Privacy

Information collected at claim time is used only to verify eligibility, pay the prize and keep required records. See the Privacy Policy.

11. General conditions

By claiming a prize, winners release the Sponsor, Apple Inc. and Google LLC from liability for any loss arising from participation or the prize, to the extent permitted by law. The Sponsor may cancel, suspend or modify the contest if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity, with notice in the app. Decisions of the Sponsor on all matters relating to the contest are final.
