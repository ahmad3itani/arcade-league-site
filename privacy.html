Privacy Policy

Last updated September 10, 2026

Who we are

Arcade League is operated by Ahmad Itani ("we", "us"). Questions or requests about this policy: ahmed3itani@gmail.com.

What we collect

By default you play under an anonymous account: a random account ID, your player name, your game runs (level seed, inputs and scores), league standings, tickets, coins, streaks, chests, duels, cosmetic items and settings. If you link Google or Apple to save your progress, we also store the email address, name and identity ID that provider shares. We never see or store passwords.

Reports and blocks

If you report a player we store the report (reason, optional note, the name reported) for review. If you block a player we store that block so it can be enforced. The player you reported or blocked is never told who you are.

Prize claims

Only if you win a cash prize and claim it, we collect your legal name, PayPal email address, country and province/state of residence, and date of birth, to confirm eligibility and pay you. This information is used for nothing else and is kept only as long as needed for payment and record-keeping obligations.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can tell you about league deadlines, promotions, duels and chests. Turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Diagnostics and analytics

When enabled, we use Sentry for crash reports (device model, OS version, stack traces — not linked to your account) and PostHog for product analytics (screens and games you use, scores, feature usage — linked to your random account ID). Our hosting provider processes IP addresses to operate and secure the service. We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Where data lives

Game and account data is stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL). Crash and analytics data is processed by Sentry and PostHog. Prizes are paid through PayPal. Each processes data under its own privacy terms.

Your rights and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Danger zone → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your profile, runs, standings, coins, streaks, chests, duels, reports you filed and any unpaid prize are erased. Analytics events already sent are deleted on request. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data, or to request deletion if you no longer have the app.

Children

Arcade League is not directed at children under 13, and cash prizes require being the age of majority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will note it in the app before the change takes effect.
